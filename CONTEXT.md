# Nomad Hub AR

A WebAR experience printed on a bag of Nomad Hub coffee: scanning the bag's QR shows a caged sparrow escaping in a capsule, and invites the viewer to leave their email for a discount.

## Language

**Sequence**:
The cage's animated story, from the sparrow coding to the open door with two feathers left behind; it replays in a loop.
_Avoid_: animation, video, timeline

**Offer**:
The invitation to leave an email in exchange for the Discount Code, shown one second after the first Sequence ends.
_Avoid_: popup, form, prompt

**Lead Source**:
Where a Lead was captured; Leads from this experience are tagged as coming from the coffee bag's AR.
_Avoid_: channel, campaign, UTM

**Consent**:
A Lead's explicit agreement to receive Nomad Hub news and promotions by email; required to receive the Discount Code.
_Avoid_: opt-in, subscription, terms

**Welcome Email**:
The email a new Lead receives with their first name and the Discount Code, so the code survives closing the experience.
_Avoid_: confirmation email, receipt

**Offer Badge**:
The small corner reminder left after the Offer is closed: it reopens the Offer, or shows the Discount Code once the email has been left.
_Avoid_: pill, button, chip

**Discount Code**:
The single code, the same for everyone, that a Lead receives in exchange for their email.
_Avoid_: coupon, voucher, promo code

**Lead**:
A person who left their first name and email in the experience, stored as a contact in the brand's mailing list.
_Avoid_: subscriber, user, customer
